**Environment:** WSL2/Linux, Node 24, Docker; PostgreSQL 16 in its own container (port 54736 from `.env.worktree`), API port 3000 and web (Vite) port 5173 as fixed by the e2e config and the Keycloak realm, Keycloak 26 (`make auth-up`); browser Chromium via Playwright; date 2026-10-06.

## 0. Gates and red evidence

**Expected:** `make ci` green; the new tests fail before the change.

**Observed:**

- ✅ `make ci` exit code 0 (gates, coverage, CRAP, build, bundle budget, conformance QG-U5).
- ✅ Red before the fix: the forced colors test listed six controls of the onboarding view without a focus indicator (`input ""`, `select "Keine Lichtzone …"`, `button "Standort anlegen"`, …), and the reduced motion test found two running animations of 500 ms when the sheet "Mehr" opened.
- ✅ Green after the fix: 15 passed, 9 skipped (each test runs in the project it belongs to: phone width, orientation and motion in "mobil"; zoom, forced colors and the self-checks in "desktop").
- ✅ Self-checks (`display-checks.spec.ts`): the measurements find a 600 px box, text cut off by a fixed height, a tooltip, a focus shown only as a box shadow in forced colors, a borderless button and a status text in its background colour.

## 1. Criterion: 320 CSS px and 400 % zoom without horizontal scrolling or cut-off content (1.4.10, 1.4.4)

**Expected:** every view at 320 px wide and at 320 × 256 px (400 % of a 1280 × 1024 window) has no sideways scroll, no element outside the viewport and no box that hides part of its content.

**Observed:**

- ✅ Test: all 13 views of a non-reviewer account, with light zones and a location named "Fensterbank im Schlafzimmer nach Südsüdwest" (`01-320px-light-*.png`, `02-320px-start-*.png`, `03-zoom-400-light-*.png`).
- ✅ The table "Einstufungsregeln" turns into one card per row below `md` (DS-24), visible in `03-zoom-400-light-desktop.png`.
- ⚠️ At 320 × 256 px the sticky header and bottom bar leave about 140 px for content; usable, but tight. Whether the bar should hide on very short viewports is a design question for US-QS-08 (focus not obscured, 2.4.11).
- ⏭️ Views of the reviewer and operator roles (`/review`, `/operator`) are not in the run; they need a role on the test account.

## 2. Criterion: user text spacing (1.4.12)

**Expected:** with line height 1.5, paragraph spacing 2, letter spacing 0.12 and word spacing 0.16 nothing overlaps or is cut off.

**Observed:**

- ✅ Test: the stylesheet is injected in every view on phone (412 px) and desktop; no layout problem (`04-text-spacing-light-*.png`). No fix was needed: no text box has a fixed height or `overflow: hidden`.

## 3. Criterion: portrait and landscape (1.3.4)

**Expected:** every view works at 412 × 915 and 915 × 412; nothing locks the orientation.

**Observed:**

- ✅ Test: both orientations over all views (`05-landscape-light-mobil.png`). There is no web manifest yet, so no `orientation` entry; the viewport meta tag allows zoom.

## 4. Criterion: reduced motion (2.3.3, DS-19)

**Expected:** with "reduce motion" animations and transitions are off or a fade.

**Observed:**

- ❌ → ✅ The sheet "Mehr" (Vaul) slid in for 500 ms regardless of the setting; Vaul injects its own unlayered styles and sets inline transitions while dragging. Fixed with an unlayered `prefers-reduced-motion` block in `tokens.css` that ends every animation and transition after 1 ms. Test: no running animation over 10 ms after opening the sheet, transition duration of a link 1 ms, Esc still closes the sheet.

## 5. Criterion: forced colors and dark scheme (1.4.11, 2.4.7)

**Expected:** focus indicator, borders of controls and status texts stay visible.

**Observed:**

- ❌ → ✅ The focus ring is a box shadow (`focus-visible:ring-2`), and every control sets `outline-none`; forced colors drop box shadows, so the focus vanished. Fixed with an unlayered `forced-colors` block in `tokens.css`: `:focus-visible` gets a 2 px `Highlight` outline (`06-forced-colors-focus-*.png`, "Bestand" focused).
- ✅ Buttons get a border in forced colors only (`forced-colors:border` in `Button` and the close button of `Dialog`), because the system replaces their fill. Fields already have a border.
- ✅ Test: in forced colors and in the dark scheme the first 12 tab stops of every view show an indicator, no button or field is without a border (forced colors), and no status or alert text has the colour of its background (`07-dark-focus-*.png`). The contrast of ring and field boundary in dark is computed by `style-contrast.test.ts`.
- ⏭️ A pass with a real Windows contrast theme (High Contrast Black/White, Edge) was not possible on this machine; only Chromium's emulation was checked.

## 6. Criterion: content on hover or focus (1.4.13)

**Expected:** a tooltip closes on Esc, stays while hovered and does not time out.

**Observed:**

- ✅ No view has a tooltip (`title` attribute or `role="tooltip"`); the test fails as soon as one appears, so it then gets a test for the three properties.
